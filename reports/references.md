## Verification log (2026-10-13)

**Bibliographic records.** All 63 DOIs listed above were resolved programmatically against the Crossref REST API
(and the AqSolDB data DOI against DataCite). For each, authors, title, venue, year, volume, issue and pages were
compared with the entry; all matched, and no entry had to be removed. The five non-DOI items were checked on their
landing pages: arXiv 2102.09548 (TDC), 1612.01474 (deep ensembles), 1704.01212 and PMLR v70 (Gilmer et al.), the
NeurIPS 2017 proceedings page (LightGBM), and the Practical Cheminformatics post (Walters 2018).

**Quantitative claims.** Each number attributed to a source in `reports/literature_review.md` and
`docs/aqsoldb_sources.md` was re-read in the abstract (Europe PMC/Crossref) or the open-access full text (Europe PMC
XML) of the cited work. Confirmed without change: AqSolDB source sizes, temperature rules, 19,796 instances /
9,982 compounds, selection rules, G1–G5 counts, solubility classes and 17 descriptors [7]; per-source SDs, AqSolPred
results (MAE 0.397 / RMSE 0.539; LOO 0.348 / 0.483) and the observed-versus-actual error argument [22]; ESOL
data size (2,874), term ranking and factor 5–8 validation [27]; ESOL coefficients as reproduced in [28];
MoleculeNet ESOL size, split, metric and best RMSEs (XGBoost 0.99, MPNN 0.58) [29]; Huuskonen 1,297 / 884 / 413,
r² 0.92, s 0.60 (ANN) and 0.88, 0.71 (MLR) [26]; GSE form and 0.7–0.8 RMSE [8], 580 validation compounds [32],
AAE 0.43 vs 0.56 on 150 compounds [36]; SolTranNet 3,393 parameters, RMSE 1.459 / 1.711, 94.8 % sensitivity
[49]; Hughes et al. logS RMSE 0.900 and Tm RMSE 52.8 °C [20]; Lovrić et al. 829 compounds, RMSE 0.70 / 0.72 [42];
Panapitiya et al. 17,149 molecules, GNN RMSE 0.91 / 1.17 vs competitor means 1.14 / 1.62 [24]; Llompart et al.
first-challenge summary, second-challenge minimum RMSE 0.70, SDi > 0.5 curation filter, ≈ 0.10 RMSE gain and
AqSolPred R² 0.56 on OChem [8]; Palmer & Mitchell 0.6–0.7 vs < 0.05 [16]; Avdeef SDi 0.15 / 0.24, × 3 factor and
0.17 over 870 molecules [19]; Attia et al. 0.58 (411 compounds, Katritzky), 0.60 (Hughes) and the 0.5–1 log
aleatoric limit [15]; Boobier et al. ≈ 70 % within ±0.7 log [25]; Saal & Petereit 465 compounds [10]; polymorph
ratio < 2 [13]; amorphous advantage 10–1,600-fold [14]; Bemis & Murcko 1,179 frameworks / 5,120 drugs / 32 [51];
